The Farm

A Kentucky family breeding program

Creek View Equine is a family-run farm in the rolling country outside Munfordville, Kentucky. We keep the program small on purpose — every horse is handled daily, started right, and known by name. Visitors are welcome by appointment; come meet the stallion before you book.
